CLL

CLL (Corners of the Last Layer) is a step in solving the 2x2 cube where all last-layer corners are simultaneously solved using one of 40 specific algorithms. Mastering CLL allows for faster solutions by eliminating the need for additional steps, making it a key technique for speedcubers aiming for efficient and advanced solves.
