2x2 EG-1

EG-1 is an advanced method for solving the 2x2 cube when the first face has a solved layer with incorrect permutation. It consists of 42 algorithms divided into 7 different subsets: S, AS, PI, U, L, T, and H. Learning EG-1 can significantly improve your solving speed by allowing you to solve the cube in just two looks.
