Advanced F2L

Advanced F2L (First Two Layers) is a method for solving the first two layers of the Rubik's Cube simultaneously. Unlike beginner F2L, which involves solving each pair one by one, Advanced F2L allows you to pair and insert the pieces in a single algorithm, improving your solve efficiency.
