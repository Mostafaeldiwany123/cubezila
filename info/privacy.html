We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, including your email address and username. We also collect data about your cube-solving times and progress when you use our timer feature.

We use the collected information to provide and improve our services, including:

  • Tracking your progress and statistics
  • Providing personalized solving suggestions
  • Communicating with you about updates and features

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. Your data is stored securely using Firebase's infrastructure.
