FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I create an account?
Click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner. You can create an account using your email address or sign in with Google. The process takes less than a minute!
Is Cubezila free to use?
Yes! Cubezila offers a free tier with access to essential features. We also offer a premium subscription with additional features like advanced statistics, custom themes, and more.

Timer & Statistics

How accurate is the timer?
Our timer is highly accurate and follows WCA (World Cube Association) standards. It measures solve times down to the millisecond and uses your device's internal clock for precision.
If I was using another timer do I have to start all over again?
No! Cubezila offers import functionality which allows you to import your solves from other timers.
Can I export my solve times?
Yes! You can export your solve times and statistics in txt format. This feature is available in the timer settings in tabs menu.
Can I customize the timer interface?
Yes, you can customize the timer display, including themes (light/dark mode), digit size, and layout preferences to suit your needs.
What statistics are available?
Our statistics page shows detailed metrics including current, best, and average solve times (ao5, ao12, ao100), solve distribution graphs, and progress tracking over time.
Can I use keyboard shortcuts?
Yes! You can use the spacebar or press the 2 shift and 2 ctrl buttons together to start/stop the timer, p to delete latest solve, r to reset history, 2 to add +2 penalty, d for DNF, Ctrl/⌘ + Z to undo deleting a solve, and we offer various keyboard shortcuts for quick access to timer functions and settings.
Can I use the timer offline?
Yes! Cubezila is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that works offline. Once you've visited the site, it will continue to work even without an internet connection. All your times are stored locally on your device.
How do I install Cubezila on my device?
You can install Cubezila as a PWA on your device. On most browsers, you'll see an "Add to Home Screen" or "Install" option in the address bar or menu. This allows you to use Cubezila like a native app with full offline support.
Will my times be saved if I'm offline?
Yes! All your solve times are automatically saved locally on your device, whether you're online or offline. When you're offline, you can still time solves, view statistics, and use most features. Your data will sync when you're back online.

Learning Resources

Where should I start as a beginner?
We recommend starting with our "Beginner's Method" tutorial series. It breaks down the solving process into easy-to-follow steps and includes video demonstrations.
How can I improve my solving speed?
Practice regularly, do a lot of solve times in our timer and check out the detailed statistics page to see how to improve.

General Questions

What tools are available on Cubezila?
Cubezila offers several useful tools including an Algorithm Inverter, Right to Left Converter, and more. These tools are designed to help you practice and improve your solving techniques.
How can I track my progress?
Our timer includes detailed statistics tracking, showing your averages, best times, and progress over time. You can view your solve history, analyze trends, and identify areas for improvement.
Do you offer learning resources for beginners?
Yes! We provide comprehensive tutorials, algorithm guides, and solving techniques suitable for all skill levels, from complete beginners to advanced speedcubers.
How do I contact support?
You can reach our support team through the contact form on our website, or email us directly at support@cubezila.com. We typically respond within 24 hours.
